Only Search: three directions, four screens

Each direction shows Home, App drawer, Search results and Calculations. Flip directions with the bar at the bottom or the arrow keys. D is the merged pick; A–C stay for reference. Calculator answers are hand-worked mocks of what SoulverCore should return; currency and crypto figures use placeholder rates.

D · Merged (current pick)

Android home with widgets from A, the bottom search bar and list results from C. Tight padding, 3–9px corners.

A · Glass Spotlight

Closest to iOS. Icon grid, search field at the top, calculator as the hero card. Familiar, but the field is far from the thumb.

B · Plain Text

No icons, all type. The answer appears inline in the input as you type, and Calculations becomes a Soulver-style scratchpad with variables.

C · Command Bar

Raycast-like. A search bar is always on the home screen, results are a keyboard-style list with a selection, and math shows as input → output with a conversion table.
